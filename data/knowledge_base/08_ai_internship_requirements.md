# AI Internship Requirements (AI-390)

All BS Artificial Intelligence students must complete one internship.

## Eligibility
- Cumulative GPA of at least 3.0.
- Completion of AI-201 Machine Learning and CS-210 Data Structures.
- At least 60 credit hours completed.

## Duration
Minimum 8 weeks and 240 working hours. The internship takes place in the summer after Semester 6.

## Deliverables
1. A signed offer letter submitted before the internship starts.
2. A supervisor evaluation form submitted by the host organization.
3. A written internship report of 10 to 15 pages, submitted within 3 weeks of the internship ending.

## Grading
The internship is graded Pass or Fail and is worth 2 credit hours. It does not affect GPA. An internship must involve AI, machine learning, or data work. Purely administrative or unrelated technical roles are not accepted.

## Approval
Students apply through the Career Office. The internship coordinator, Dr. Amna Rahman, approves each placement. Internships completed without prior approval are not credited.
