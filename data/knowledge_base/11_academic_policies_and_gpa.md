# Academic Policies and GPA Requirements

## GPA scale
NIT uses a 4.0 GPA scale.

## Minimum GPA thresholds
- 2.5 cumulative GPA is required to graduate.
- 3.0 cumulative GPA is required to remain in good standing in the AI program, to enroll in AI-390 Internship, and to enroll in AI electives.
- 3.5 cumulative GPA is required for the Dean's Honor List and for the Honors Thesis option.

## Academic probation
A student whose cumulative GPA falls below 3.0 is placed on academic probation for one semester. If the GPA is not raised to 3.0 by the end of that semester, the student is moved to the general Computer Science program.

## Course repetition
A course can be repeated at most twice. Only the highest grade counts toward the GPA.

## Attendance
Students must attend at least 75 percent of classes in each course to be allowed to sit the final exam.

## Scholarships
The Merit Scholarship covers 50 percent of tuition and requires a cumulative GPA of at least 3.7.
