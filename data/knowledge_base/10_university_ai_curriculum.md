# NIT BS Artificial Intelligence Curriculum

The BS Artificial Intelligence program at Northfield Institute of Technology (NIT) takes 8 semesters and requires 130 credit hours to graduate.

## Year 1
- Semester 1: CS-101 Programming Fundamentals (Python), MATH-101 Calculus I, AI-101 Introduction to AI.
- Semester 2: MATH-110 Discrete Mathematics, CS-102 Object-Oriented Programming.

## Year 2
- Semester 3: AI-201 Machine Learning, MATH-201 Linear Algebra, STAT-201 Probability and Statistics.
- Semester 4: CS-210 Data Structures, DS-210 Data Science Tools, CS-220 Databases.

## Year 3
- Semester 5: AI-301 Deep Learning.
- Semester 6: AI-310 Natural Language Processing, AI-320 Computer Vision.
- Summer after Semester 6: AI-390 Internship.

## Year 4
- Semester 7: AI-330 MLOps and Deployment, AI-401 Capstone Project I.
- Semester 8: AI-402 Capstone Project II and two AI electives.

## Electives
Reinforcement Learning, Generative AI, Speech Processing, and AI Ethics.
