# Computer Vision (AI-320)

Computer vision enables machines to extract meaning from images and video.

## Common tasks
- Image classification: assign one label to an image.
- Object detection: locate and label multiple objects with bounding boxes.
- Semantic segmentation: label every pixel.

## Key models
CNN architectures such as ResNet, detectors such as YOLO, and Vision Transformers (ViT).

## Datasets and metrics
Popular datasets are MNIST, ImageNet, and COCO. Detection is evaluated with Intersection over Union (IoU) and mean Average Precision (mAP).

## Tools
OpenCV for image processing, and PyTorch with torchvision for model training.

## Course details at NIT
AI-320 Computer Vision is worth 3 credit hours and is taken in Semester 6. The prerequisite is AI-301. The course project is to train an object detector on a custom dataset.
