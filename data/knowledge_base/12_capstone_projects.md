# Capstone Projects (AI-401 and AI-402)

The capstone is a two-semester project completed in Semesters 7 and 8.

## Eligibility
Students must have completed AI-301 and the AI-390 Internship, and hold a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0.

## Team size
Teams of 2 to 4 students.

## Deliverables
A project proposal, a midterm demonstration, a final report, a public GitHub repository, and a 15-minute final demonstration.

## Grading breakdown
- Proposal: 10 percent
- Midterm demonstration: 20 percent
- Final report: 30 percent
- Final demonstration: 30 percent
- Peer evaluation: 10 percent

## Past project examples
Crop disease detection using computer vision, Urdu sentiment analysis, and a hospital policy chatbot built with retrieval-augmented generation.

## Honors Thesis option
Students with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 may replace the capstone with an individual Honors Thesis supervised by a faculty member.
