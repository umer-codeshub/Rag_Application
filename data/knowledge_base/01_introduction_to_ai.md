# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI-101)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of building computer systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence, such as perception, reasoning, language understanding, and decision making.

## Types of AI
- Narrow AI: systems built for one specific task, such as spam filtering or image recognition. Every AI system in use today is narrow AI.
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI): a hypothetical system with human-level ability across all tasks. It does not exist yet.

## Major subfields
Machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and knowledge representation.

## Brief history
The term "artificial intelligence" was coined at the Dartmouth workshop in 1956. Funding collapsed during two "AI winters" in the 1970s and late 1980s. The modern boom began around 2012, when deep neural networks trained on GPUs won major image recognition competitions.

## Course details at NIT
AI-101 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence is the first AI course in the BS Artificial Intelligence program. It is taken in Semester 1, is worth 3 credit hours, and has no prerequisites. Assessment consists of 2 quizzes, 1 midterm exam, and a final project. The course uses Python.
